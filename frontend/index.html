Questions, answered

Is the AI real in this version?

This frontend ships in demo mode: the transcript and extraction are clearly labelled sample data. Your microphone recording and file upload are real. Connect a transcription backend by setting one value in js/env.js.

Which browsers can record audio?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Recording needs HTTPS or localhost; in any other case you can still upload a file.

Can I change what the system extracted?

Yes. Every extracted field is editable and nothing is priced until you confirm.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The recorder, tables and navigation are built for touch screens and small displays.

Where is my data stored?

In demo mode, only in your browser’s local storage. With a backend connected, data lives wherever your API stores it.
